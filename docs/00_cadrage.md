# RetailPulse — Scope at a glance

RetailPulse is a fictional internal retail operations and data platform. It integrates operational and supplier information and exposes reliable, actionable workflows to catalog staff, logistics teams, operators, and internal applications.

The platform is **not** a consumer storefront, a checkout/payment gateway, or a full ERP. The dataset is synthetic, and the simulator represents upstream systems rather than finished product functionality.

For the authoritative definition of actors, behaviors, boundaries, and user-facing requirements, see the [product specification](product_spec.md).

For the implementation approach and technology responsibilities, see [architecture](architecture.md). For current progress and the next useful milestone, see the [roadmap](01_roadmap.md).
